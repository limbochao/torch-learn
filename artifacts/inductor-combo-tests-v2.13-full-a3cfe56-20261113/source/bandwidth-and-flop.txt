 1273:     def estimate_kernel_num_bytes(self):

 1278:         far we are from the peak memory bandwidth. It's important that
 1279:         we want to avoid overestimating the sizes of the inputs and outputs,

 1281:         which may be even larger than the theoretical bandwidth and thus
 1282:         become very misleading. This is particularly problematic for cases
